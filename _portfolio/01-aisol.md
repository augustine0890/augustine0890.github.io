**Champion · FPT AI Hackathon 3 (2025)** — *Global competition across FPT offices worldwide · Track: KT/KB Technical Solutions*

Team **Cẩm Y Vệ (FKR-KTX)** won 1st place with **AISOL**, a Japanese-language enterprise RAG platform. The pipeline converts **Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and PDF documents** into structured inputs for AI retrieval.

### Key Contributions

- Designed and delivered document ingestion and processing across heterogeneous business document formats.
- Parallelized processing of **text, tables, and diagrams**, addressing the sequential bottleneck of conventional document loaders.
- Preserved **semantic context across document structures** to support queries spanning multiple sections.
- Integrated **Qdrant** for vector storage, **Gemini** for multimodal understanding, and **Qwen** for Japanese-language generation.

### Tech Stack

`Python` · `Qdrant` · `Gemini` · `Qwen` · `RAG` · `Vector Search` · `Multimodal AI` · `Japanese NLP`
